# corpus — LLM Wiki

## What this is

The knowledge corpus. Everything the system knows, in two tiers.

**canon/** — Locked. Operator verbatim word, anchored artifact, build state declared. Never modify without operator sign-off.

**pending/** — Staging area. Transcripts, manuals, operator build docs, raw PDFs awaiting triage and `.md` sibling generation. Material here may contain false claims — read with Rule 6 (Designed ≠ Built) in mind.
